## Replicated Games

### Color Roller Splat
In **Color Roller Splat**, you're in charge of splashing vibrant colors across a grid of white tiles by moving your ball across the board! With a simple swipe of your finger, guide the ball up, down, left, and right to cover every tile in beautiful paint. But it’s not as easy as it sounds—plan your moves carefully to ensure you paint every tile while avoiding obstacles and walls that block your path.

**Features:**
- **Swipe to Paint**: Move the ball in any direction to leave a colorful trail behind.
- **Challenging Levels**: Each level introduces new puzzles, requiring you to think strategically about your moves.
- **Smooth Controls**: Enjoy responsive and fluid ball movement as you tackle more complex patterns and obstacles.

*Are you ready to color the world one swipe at a time? Let's play Color Roller Splat!*

### Duet Game Replica
Experience the thrilling challenge of **Duet** in this replicated project! Players control two synchronized balls and must navigate through obstacles while maintaining their formation. This project emphasizes timing and coordination, offering a fun and engaging experience.

**Features:**
- Unique gameplay mechanics involving dual controls.
- Increasingly difficult levels.
- Simple, minimalist design focusing on gameplay.

---

### Tic Tac Toe Replica
A classic game of **Tic Tac Toe**, where two players take turns marking a space in a 3x3 grid. The goal is to get three of your marks in a row—horizontally, vertically, or diagonally. This project showcases basic game mechanics, user input handling, and win condition checking.

**Features:**
- Two-player mode (Player vs. Player).
- Clear interface and simple gameplay.
- Ability to restart the game.
